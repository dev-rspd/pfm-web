# CreditRichie

CreditRichie is a personal financial management application for tracking credit card balances, statement dates, due dates, and payments: optionally linking financial accounts through our supported third-party aggregator, Plaid, to automatically populate this information. It is developed and operated by an individual developer, for individual and family use.
